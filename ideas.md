# CYBERPUNK MEGAPOLIS — Design Exploration

## Three directions considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| **Aerial Transit Noir** | A rain-slick dusk city is read through the language of elevated transit maps, signal lamps, and nocturnal infrastructure. The player is a living disruption moving through civic machinery. | 0.07 |
| **Industrial Shrine** | Monumental concrete, ritual geometry, and volcanic amber light make the metropolis feel like an old machine city rediscovering electricity. | 0.04 |
| **Chromatic Slum Opera** | Dense signs, patched housing, and saturated consumer color make the skyline lively, intimate, and unruly rather than sleek. | 0.09 |

## Chosen direction: Aerial Transit Noir

### Design Movement

**Neo-futurist transit cartography with high-contrast urban brutalism.** The city is a layered circulation machine: rail lines, skybridges, rooftop routes, service shafts, and street canyons create a readable vertical language for fast movement.

### Core Principles

1. **Velocity is legible.** Cool speed trails, a live trajectory reticle, and a camera that pulls back at velocity make motion understandable without clutter.
2. **Density has hierarchy.** Large landmark slabs establish distant silhouettes; repeated mid-rise cells and warm windows eliminate visual emptiness at street level.
3. **Infrastructure is gameplay.** Every rail, bridge, antenna, and tower cap is both world texture and a credible traversal anchor.
4. **The HUD is a flight instrument.** Information is edge-aligned and compositional rather than floating in generic centered panels.

### Color Philosophy

An ink-blue foundation gives the dusk city depth, while **Signal Cyan** functions as the ownable navigation color: it marks valid lines, anchors, player technology, and active systems. Amber counterbalances it as municipal warmth—windows, caution points, and progress. Magenta is restricted to scarce signal anomalies so the palette stays controlled rather than indiscriminately neon.

### Layout Paradigm

The game frame is organized as a **diagonal flight corridor**. The world carries most of the screen. HUD information docks into the upper-left signal band, lower-left velocity instrument, and lower-right mission rail, leaving the central and upper-right field unobstructed for the target arc and city depth.

### Signature Elements

* A curved, segmented **web trajectory line** that begins at the active hand and terminates at a physically placed city anchor.
* **Signal brackets:** thin cyan corner brackets that gather around selected anchors and key HUD meters.
* **Transit ribbons:** luminous elevated loops and service rails that stitch the skyline together at several heights.

### Interaction Philosophy

Input has decisive, tactile feedback. A target only becomes bright when it has an unobstructed world-space path. Swing, zip, wall-run, and dive announce their state through a concise two-word system readout and through the player silhouette itself.

### Animation

Camera and interface motion are damped and directional, never decorative. The city scrolls more aggressively as speed rises; web lines ease in with a brief tension pulse. UI state changes use 120–220ms opacity and transform transitions. Reduced-motion preference removes nonessential reticle and scan-line motion.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the robust humanist display family for titles, speed telemetry, and menus; **IBM Plex Mono** supplies compact machine-readable labels. Headings use upper-case tracking, while instructions remain sentence case at a comfortable size.

### Brand Essence

**CYBERPUNK MEGAPOLIS is a high-velocity urban traversal game for players who want a dense city to read, bend, and cross—not merely admire.**

Personality: **charged, infrastructural, precise.**

### Brand Voice

The voice is concise and operational, like a trusted movement system speaking from inside the suit. It avoids generic recruitment language and treats every prompt as a tactical callout.

> “Find the line. Borrow the skyline.”

> “Anchor confirmed. Momentum preserved.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a wide, cut-corner stencil with the crossbar of the **A** replaced by a rising arc. The mark is a sharp **moth-spider hybrid**: symmetry implies engineered agility, while its amber core is the single source of warm color.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Cyan — `#43F6E8`**. It is reserved for intended player agency: active anchors, applied tension, quality indicators, and selected controls.

## Style Decisions

The review refinements are accepted as an execution contract. Every game phase now carries a diagonal trajectory overlay, edge-docked flight instruments, cyan anchor brackets, and a physically implied destination marker. Signal Cyan remains the controlled agency color; amber is restricted to municipal warmth and caution. The voice remains operational and movement-first: **“Find the line. Borrow the skyline.”**
